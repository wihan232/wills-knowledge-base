## Significant Historical Figures


### John Locke

![[locke2.jpg]]

The term [[concept-authority|argumentum ad verecundiam]], meaning appeal to modesty, was first coined by the world famous English philosopher and physician, John Locke. He wrote extensively about what we now know as appeal to authority in his 1690 work, _An Essay Concerning Human Understanding_.

Locke understood that _ad verecundiam_ was a persuasive technique instead of tool that helps reach logical conclusions. It's important to note that he recognized the fallacious nature of the appeal to authority, noting that it can be used to intimidate others and sway opinions.

>Whoever backs his tenets with such authorities ‘men, whose parts, learning, eminency, power or some other cause has gained a name’
 ...This I think, may be called _argumentum ad verecundiam_.

-John Locke, _An Essay Concerning Human Knowledge_ pg. 253
