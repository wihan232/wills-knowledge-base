### Social Media

Speaking with the intent of establishing speaker authority is a common characteristic for the tactic of [[concept-authority|appeal to authority]]. With the growth of social media platforms, politicians and influencers have shifted their discourse style to cater towards the social media land scape. While orators have [[history-of-authority|historically]] spoken on issues on the podium, the modern day campaigns have often demanded the speaker have an online presence.

Rosette-Crake's study on social media discourse points out that social media oratory is inherently authoritative due to the fact that the format naturally highlights the reliability associated with spoken communication by a recognizable speaker.
