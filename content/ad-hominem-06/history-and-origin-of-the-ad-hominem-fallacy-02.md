## Significant Historical Figures

### Aristotle

There have been many famous figures in the field of philosophy have contributed in analyzing and establishing the boundaries of what we consider to be the types of ad-hominem fallacy. Records of ad-hominem being explicitly mentioned have dated back as early as Ancient Greek periods with Aristotle laying out the groundwork for the concept of ad-hominem being a personal attack towards someone rather than the argument itself.
