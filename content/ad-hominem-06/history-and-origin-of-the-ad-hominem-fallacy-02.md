## Significant Historical Figures

There have been many famous figures in the field of philosophy have contributed in analyzing and establishing the boundaries of what we consider to be the types of ad-hominem fallacy.
### Aristotle
![[aristotle.jpg]]
Records of ad-hominem being explicitly mentioned have dated back as early as Ancient Greek periods with Aristotle laying out the groundwork for the concept of ad-hominem being a personal attack towards someone rather than refuting the argument itself. 


## Locke

![[locke.jpg]]

Known for his writing in philosophical concepts, such as his book, Of the Conduct of the Understanding, and many more, Locke contributed to the definition of the ad-hominem fallacy.
