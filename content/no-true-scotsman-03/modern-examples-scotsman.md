## Relevance in Modern Day

[[concept-scotsman|No true Scotsman]] is one of the lesser used and known fallacies in modern day discourse. Nevertheless, it can still be useful to know its range of use cases to know how and when it's used.

# Real Life Examples

#### No True Muslim

In the wake of 9/11, President George W. Bush distinguishes between the actions of extremist organization and the teachings of Islam: 

>"It's teachings are good and peaceful, and those who commit evil in the name of Allah blaspheme the name of Allah. The terrorists are traitors to their own faith, trying, in effect, to hijack Islam itself."


Similarly, Barack Obama asserted in 2015:

> "The terrorists do not speak for over a billion Muslims who reject their hateful ideology."

>Nor should we grant these terrorists the religious legitimacy that they seek. They are not religious leaders — they’re terrorists.  And we are not at war with Islam. We are at war with people who have perverted Islam.


In essence, both presidents' speeches aimed to define the actions of the extremist group and what it means to be a Muslim as separate from one another. However, the notion of what constitutes a "true Muslim" is based off the assumptions of the arguer, making it closer to a generalization of a group.
