### Concept of Hasty Generalization Fallacy

The hasty generalization fallacy is an informal fallacy wherein a person uses a single example or weak examples to arrive at a conclusion. The name is based on the fact that the individual uses the logical shortcut of generalizing an entire population or group of people from a small sample size.

This particular fallacy can also occur in contexts of statistics and research. Studies generally require a large enough sample size because sample size too small would not lead to logical conclusions about the study's population.
