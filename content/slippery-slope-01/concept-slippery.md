## Concept of Slippery Slope Fallacy

The slippery slope fallacy is a logical shortcut that occurs when an arguer claims that a series of consequences will arise from an event or a position. The arguer often makes it appear that the consequences are likely or certain to happen.

Slippery slope is [[modern-examples-slippery|often used]] to create fear by exaggerating the likelihood of the domino effect occurring or by making the consequences seem more dire than they really are. [[history-of-slippery|Douglas N. Walton]] argued that an essential characteristic is the sense of a "loss of control" highlighted by arguers using this tactic.
