## Relevance in Modern Day

The [[concept-slippery|slippery slope fallacy]] has been used extensively in everyday life from small talk to political commentaries. Their effectiveness comes from arguers taking advantage of people who are prone to slippery slope arguments

## Real Life Examples

The basic slippery slope argument can be as mundane as scolding someone about their diet:

>“If you eat one cookie today, tomorrow you’ll want to eat ten, and before you know it, you’ll have gained 10 pounds.”


Others can take the form of political discourse:

>If we lower the drinking age, next thing we know kids will get to drive at age ten, and vote at fifteen.
