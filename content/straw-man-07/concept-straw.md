## Concept of Straw Man Fallacies

The Straw man is an informal fallacy that occurs when an arguer refutes an argument that is not the same as the one highlighted in a discussion. Here, the arguer tries to gain the advantage of appearing to refute an opponent's claim by refuting a completely different claim. It's important that the straw man user doesn't acknowledge that they aren't engaging with the opponent's proposition.

Arguably, straw man is one of the most popular and common argumentative tactics used throughout [[history-of-straw|history]]. It is the tactic of putting words into the opponent's mouth, and making the opponent's position appear weaker out of context.
